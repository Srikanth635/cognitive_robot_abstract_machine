The pouring frame specialises the action core's frame for pouring, CRAM-AICOR's
aicor:Pouring: an agent makes a substance leave a source over a part of it, such as its
lip, and enter a goal through a part of it, such as its opening, so that the substance
accumulates in the goal.

It also names the situations a pour is performed in: the material poured, liquid,
granular or cohesive, and the reason the pour ends, such as the wanted quantity being in
the goal or the source being empty. Each of them defines a variant of the frame.
